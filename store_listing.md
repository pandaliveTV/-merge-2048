### Full description
Merge 2048 is the classic number puzzle, rebuilt with smooth animations and a few modern extras.

Swipe up, down, left or right to slide the tiles. When two tiles with the same number touch, they merge into one. Keep merging until you reach 2048, then keep going for a higher score.

**What's inside**
- **Three board sizes.** Start on the classic 4×4. Level up to unlock the Big 5×5 and Huge 6×6 boards.
- **Daily Challenge.** Everyone in the world plays the same board each day. Try to set your best score and collect a bonus.
- **Undo.** Take back a move you regret. You get 3 free undos per game.
- **Hints.** Stuck? A smart hint shows you the best next move.
- **Second chance.** Out of moves? Continue by clearing the four smallest tiles.
- **Tile themes.** Choose Classic, Midnight, Ocean, Candy, Forest or Neon.
- **Best scores** are saved separately for every board.
- **Auto-save.** Leave any time and pick up where you left off.
- **Plays offline.** No internet needed to play.
- **English and Arabic.**
- Light haptics and sound effects, each with its own toggle.

Easy to learn and hard to master. It works for a quick break or a long session.

Merge 2048 is free to play and contains ads. You can remove banner and interstitial ads with a one-time purchase.
